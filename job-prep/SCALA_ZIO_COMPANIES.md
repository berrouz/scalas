## 💡 Tips for Job Search

### For Scala + Cats Effect
1. **Most common**: Finance, E-commerce, Data platforms
2. **Keywords**: "Functional programming", "Typelevel", "Cats"
3. **Cities**: London, Berlin, Amsterdam, Warsaw, Zürich

### For ZIO Specifically
1. **Smaller market** but growing fast
2. **Look for**: "New project", "Greenfield", "Modern stack"
3. **Startups**: More likely to choose ZIO
4. **Ask in interview**: "Planning to adopt ZIO?"

### Red Flags
- "Scala like Java" - avoid
- No mention of FP libraries - likely legacy
- "Switching from Java" - might be painful transition
- No testing culture - quality issues

### Green Flags
- "Cats Effect" or "ZIO" in job description
- "Tagless Final", "Free monad", "MTL" mentioned
- Active open-source contributions
- Conference speakers on team
- Training budget mentioned
- Clear progression path

## 🎓 Interview Preparation

Based on real interviews, companies look for:

### Must Know
1. **Deep FP understanding** - not just syntax
2. **Monad/Applicative theory** - laws and use cases
3. **Error handling** - typed errors, accumulation
4. **Concurrency** - Fibers, Ref, Deferred
5. **Testing** - property-based, effectful code

### Nice to Have
1. **Type-level programming** - Shapeless, refined types
2. **Streaming** - FS2, Akka Streams
3. **Distributed systems** - Saga, Outbox, CDC
4. **Performance** - JVM tuning, profiling

### Differentiators (Senior+)
1. **Architectural patterns** - Event sourcing, CQRS
2. **Category theory** - Natural transformations, Yoneda
3. **Compiler knowledge** - Macros, plugins
4. **Production experience** - Real war stories

## ⚠️ Important Notes

**Market Reality**:
- High demand for senior Scala developers
- Very hard for companies to find qualified candidates
- Strong negotiation position for experienced developers
- Remote work increasingly accepted
- Salaries competitive with other languages

**For ZIO Specifically**:
- Smaller but passionate community
- Growing enterprise adoption
- Easier learning curve than Cats Effect (debatable)
- Better IDE support (typed errors help)
- More "batteries included"

**Career Path**:
- Scala developers are in demand
- Easy to switch companies
- Many opportunities in fintech
- Remote work more common than other JVM languages
- Strong community support
